Why We Exist
A co-founder bought a Cimarron 1851 Navy. The hammer broke. The replacement arrived oversized. The gunsmith cost more than the part.

That one repair exposed a pattern across the American firearms market. Legacy manufacturers charge premium prices for products with known, decades old engineering flaws, and nobody has fixed them.

The Thompson drum has jammed since 1919. The 1851 replacement hammer has needed a gunsmith since 1851. Continental takes the most beloved designs in American history, applies modern manufacturing discipline, and sells them at prices real shooters can pay.

Four Engineering Principles

If it needs fitting, the design has failed.

Every Continental part is held to the same four rules, from a flintlock frizzen spring to a Thompson drum ratchet.

01

Timing by Dimension

Correct timing comes from machined geometry alone. No stoning, no hand files, no gunsmith adjustment.

02

CNC Repeatability

A recoil shield cut by hand takes an hour. Our fixture does it in twelve minutes, identically, every time.

03

Standard Drop-In Parts

Springs, pins and internals designed to be replaced. Easier service and a lower cost of ownership.

04

Priced for Shooters

Direct to consumer, efficient manufacturing, and no importer markup on a 150 year old design.
