Why We Exist
A co-founder bought a Cimarron 1851 Navy. The hammer broke. The replacement arrived oversized. The gunsmith cost more than the part.

That one repair exposed a pattern across the American firearms market. Legacy manufacturers charge premium prices for products with known, decades old engineering flaws, and nobody has fixed them.

The Thompson drum has jammed since 1919. The 1851 replacement hammer has needed a gunsmith since 1851. A $2,500 carbine costs $500 to build. Continental takes the most beloved designs in American history, applies modern manufacturing discipline, and sells them at prices real people can pay.

Two founders. One in finance, brand and commercial strategy. One in mechanical engineering and CNC manufacturing. Headquartered in Florida.

The Four Engineering Principles

If a Continental part needs fitting to work, the design has failed.

Timing by Machined Dimensions

Correct timing comes from geometry alone. No stoning, no hand files, no gunsmith adjustment at installation.

CNC Repeatability

Every operation that can run on a mill or lathe does. A recoil shield cut by hand takes an hour. Our fixture does it in twelve minutes, identically, every time.

Standardized Drop-In Parts

Springs, pins and internals designed for replaceability wherever safe. Easier service, lower cost of ownership, scalable production.

Accessible Pricing

Priced for working shooters, not collectors. Direct to consumer, manufacturing efficiency and no importer markup on a 150 year old design.
